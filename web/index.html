Governed field AI systems

Field-Operated AI, Bounded at the Device

Myotosis is a security-bounded architecture for field-operated AI tools running on mobile devices. It brings device-local context into AI workflows through explicit capability grants, local policy enforcement, operator consent, and auditable execution.

Problem

Cloud-first AI breaks down in the field.

Field environments introduce conditions that cloud-centric AI systems do not handle well: intermittent connectivity, ambiguous network trust, physical proximity to sensitive data, and higher consequences for misuse.

In these environments, the device cannot be treated as a thin client and the cloud cannot be treated as the final authority.

Intermittent or absent connectivity

Field workflows need local continuity when the network is slow, degraded, or unavailable.

Higher consequence environments

The cost of ambiguous authority, silent failure, or data leakage is materially higher in regulated and operational settings.

Human approval cannot be decorative

Consent must be specific, bounded, and visible to the operator when device-side actions touch sensitive data or actuation.

Core shift

Reverse the default trust model.

Myotosis treats the mobile device as a governed execution node rather than a passive endpoint. Capabilities are bounded. Invocation is explicit. Policy is enforced locally. The cloud coordinates, but does not silently inherit authority over field-side actions.

01

Resolve scope

The control plane routes requests and defines bounded scope.

02

Evaluate locally

The device validates policy, consent, and capability constraints before tool execution.

03

Execute or deny

Actions either proceed within policy or fail closed with a visible, attributable outcome.

04

Record and sync intentionally

Audit is recorded as part of normal operation, and sync remains an explicit, controlled action.

Safety model

Built for operational safety, not capability expansion.

The architecture is centered on risk containment. Devices, agents, and tools are untrusted by default. Authority is established only through scoped capability grants, cryptographic provenance, local enforcement, and immutable audit trails.

The system enforces Local policy evaluation before execution
The system does not allow Remote bypass of the policy engine
The system contains Failure to the affected device and visible workflow state
The system requires Explicit consent where policy or context demands it
The system records Attributable audit events as part of normal operation

Field conditions

Designed for real operating environments.

Myotosis is shaped around environments where connectivity is unreliable, conditions are time-sensitive, and operator clarity matters. The architecture is intended for settings where mobile devices interact with sensitive data, physical processes, or regulated workflows.

Healthcare

Bedside capture, clinical workflows, and attributable approval.

Transportation

Mobile coordination under inconsistent connectivity and varied trust zones.

Security

Bounded device actions where policy and operator visibility are mandatory.

Defense and field services

Operational environments where degraded conditions are expected, not exceptional.

Current state

Design system first. Proof later.

Myotosis currently exists as an RFC-backed design system and sandbox for governed agentic SDLC through Anthesis. The current work defines architectural constraints, threat models, policy semantics, audit requirements, distributed execution behavior, and consent flows.

It does not claim production validation yet. The value here is architectural rigor, not invented proof.
