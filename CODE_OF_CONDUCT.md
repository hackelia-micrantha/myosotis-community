# Community code of conduct

Myosotis Community expects professional, respectful, technically honest participation.

## Expected conduct

Participants should:

- discuss ideas, evidence, and trade-offs rather than attacking people;
- state uncertainty, conflicts of interest, and material provenance;
- respect privacy, embargoes, security reports, healthcare-data boundaries, and disclosure limits;
- give constructive, actionable review feedback;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, sexualized conduct, and targeted hostility;
- avoid knowingly misleading claims about compatibility, security, authorship, clinical evidence, regulatory status, or certification;
- respect maintainer decisions while using the documented review path for disagreements.

## Unacceptable conduct

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, doxxing, publication of private reports or personal/health data, deliberate disruption, repeated bad-faith argument, plagiarism, license evasion, and pressure to disclose private or exploit-sensitive material.

## Enforcement and reporting

For ordinary community concerns, contact `myosotis@micrantha.com`.

When a conduct concern includes a vulnerability, private security evidence, credentials, personal information, or other material that should not be disclosed publicly, use the private reporting path in `SECURITY.md`.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, request changes, restrict participation, lock conversations, or block accounts based on severity and pattern. Enforcement should be proportionate and avoid unnecessary disclosure.
